# Support — Snapshot Templates for Jira

We're glad to help. Most questions are answered in the
[User Guide](./user-guide.md).

## Get help

- **Email:** [support@davidleetech.uk](mailto:support@davidleetech.uk?subject=Snapshot%20Templates%20support)
- **Report a bug or request a feature:**
  [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/daviddesu/policies/issues)
- **Report a security issue:** see the
  [Security Policy](./security-policy.md).

## Response times

We aim to acknowledge support emails within **2 business days**. Please
include your Jira site URL, the affected template or issue keys, and what you
expected to happen.

## Service status

Snapshot Templates runs entirely on Atlassian Forge, so the app's
availability follows Atlassian's platform. Live status:
[developer.status.atlassian.com](https://developer.status.atlassian.com/).
